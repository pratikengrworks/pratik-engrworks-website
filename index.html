ABOUT THE COMPANY

A focused manufacturing partner for industrial lifting systems.

Pratik Engineering Works is a manufacturer of high-quality EOT crane components, delivering reliable and durable solutions for industrial lifting and material handling applications.

With a manufacturing base in Ambad MIDC, Nashik, the company focuses on precision engineering, quality workmanship, and customer satisfaction. Products are designed for demanding industrial requirements and long-term performance in heavy-duty environments.

RAIL CLAMP

Rail Clamps

Precision-engineered components designed to securely fasten crane rails in EOT crane systems. Built for firm grip, structural stability and long-term performance under heavy operational loads.

  • Single-bolt and double-bolt configurations
  • Multiple rail sizes
  • M16 and M20 hex-bolt options

CABLE TROLLEY

Heavy-Duty Moving Cable Trolleys

Durable cable-management solutions for smooth movement and support of power and control cables in EOT crane systems, helping reduce wear and tear during operation.

  • Single, double-decker and three-decker variants
  • 100–310 mm cable carrier lengths
  • 50–70 mm wheel diameter options

Double Decker Cable Trolley
